# Tronixora VPN for Android

**[Download the app](https://github.com/mmTareque01/tronixora-vpn-releases/releases/latest/download/TronixoraVPN.apk)** (newest version)

1. Download `TronixoraVPN.apk` on your Android phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
2. Open Tronixora VPN and **sign up with your e-mail** and a password.
3. Wait until we approve your account. Then tap **Connect**.

Already have an account? Tap **Log in** instead of signing up.

Works on 64-bit Android phones (almost every phone from 2017 on). Older versions are under [Releases](https://github.com/mmTareque01/tronixora-vpn-releases/releases).

Tronixora VPN is based on [sing-box](https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box) (GPL-3.0). If you received this app, you can ask us for its source code.
