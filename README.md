# Tronixora VPN

**[Download the Android app](https://github.com/mmTareque01/tronixora-vpn-releases/releases/latest/download/TronixoraVPN.apk)** (newest version)

1. Download `TronixoraVPN.apk` on your Android phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
2. Open Tronixora VPN and **sign up with your e-mail** and a password, or **log in** if you already have an account.
3. Once your account is approved or your plan is added, tap **Connect**.

**Buy a plan:** [tronixora.com/products/tronixora-vpn/buy](https://tronixora.com/products/tronixora-vpn/buy). Pay with WeChat Pay. Buying also creates your account, so you can do it before installing the app.

**Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone and iPad:** your plan works there too. Get your personal link and the steps at [tronixora.com/products/tronixora-vpn/devices](https://tronixora.com/products/tronixora-vpn/devices).

**Help:** [hello@tronixora.com](mailto:hello@tronixora.com) · [tronixora.com](https://tronixora.com/products/tronixora-vpn)
